**Ember Pact** is an original duel card battler. You face the Ash Warden — 30 health
each, mana that grows every turn, a 20-card deck of 14 unique cards (attacks,
guards, heals, draw and burn). Play cards with mouse or keys 1–7, end the turn
with Space, and be the first to bring the rival to zero. Wins and losses are
kept in localStorage; sound is a tiny WebAudio synth, no files.

Run it: `node serve.ts`, open http://localhost:8080. Screenshots are staged
deterministically with `?shot=start|play|win|lose&seed=1` (see shots.json).
Hand it in: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.
